---
title: The Shape of Calm Interfaces
summary: Notes on using whitespace, rhythm, and subtle motion in UI.
date: 2026-03-27
tags: design, typography, ux
---

Calm interfaces are usually made from restraint.

Instead of adding more components, I focus on rhythm:

- generous spacing,
- clear hierarchy,
- one accent color,
- one purposeful animation.

Typography does most of the heavy lifting. A serif display paired with a neutral body
font gives the page personality without noise.
